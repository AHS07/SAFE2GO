# Slopes, stability and site conditions

Rollovers and tip-overs are rare, but they are among the most serious events on site. They usually start with a combination of factors: a slope, a load, soft ground, and a turn. Good planning removes most of them.

## Know your tilt limit

Each machine has a maximum safe side tilt. SAFE2GO compares the measured tilt to that limit.

- At 80% of the limit: WARNING. You are close to the edge of safe operation.
- At 100% of the limit: CRITICAL. Stop and reduce the angle before doing anything else.

## Working on slopes

1. Travel straight up or down a slope where possible, not across it.
2. On an excavator, keep the tracks square to the slope and the heavy end uphill.
3. Keep the bucket or attachment low when travelling on a slope.
4. Avoid turning on a slope. If you must turn, do it slowly on the flattest ground available.
5. Do not swing a loaded bucket to the downhill side.

## Ground conditions

Soft ground, fresh fill, and trench edges can give way under the weight of the machine.

- Keep back from the edge of excavations and trenches. The ground near the edge carries the most load and is the most likely to collapse.
- Check for soft spots after rain.
- Watch for buried services and voids.

## Visibility

Fog, dust, heavy rain, and darkness reduce how far you can see. SAFE2GO raises a WARNING when visibility falls below 100 m and a CRITICAL alert below 50 m.

- Slow down and increase the distance to other machines and people.
- Use work lights and keep windows and cameras clean.
- Stop work if you cannot see the work area or your spotter.

## Heat

High cab and ambient temperatures cause fatigue and slow reactions. A WARNING appears at 35 C and a CRITICAL alert at 40 C.

- Drink water regularly, before you feel thirsty.
- Use the cab air conditioning and keep doors closed while it runs.
- Take short breaks in the shade. Tell your supervisor if you feel dizzy, confused, or sick.

## ROPS

The rollover protective structure only protects you if you stay inside it with the seatbelt fastened. If the machine starts to tip, stay seated, hold on, and do not jump.
