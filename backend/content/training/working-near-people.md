# Working near people

Being struck by a moving machine is one of the most common causes of serious injury on construction sites. Most of these events happen at low speed, close to the machine, when the operator did not know someone was there.

## Know your blind spots

Every machine has areas the operator cannot see from the seat, even with mirrors and cameras. On excavators the rear counterweight and the side opposite the boom are the largest. On loaders the area directly behind the machine and close in front of a raised bucket are hard to see.

- Walk around the machine before starting and after any break.
- Adjust mirrors and clean camera lenses at the start of the shift.
- Assume someone may be in a blind spot until you have checked.

## Swing radius

The rear of an excavator sweeps a circle when the upper structure turns. A person standing inside that circle can be struck or pinned against another object.

- Keep the swing area clear before you rotate.
- Ask the site to mark the swing area with barriers or cones when people work nearby.
- Never swing a load over people or over a truck cab with the driver inside.

## Use a spotter

A spotter is a trained person who guides you when you cannot see clearly. Agree on hand signals before work starts.

1. Keep the spotter in sight at all times.
2. Stop immediately if you lose sight of the spotter.
3. Only follow signals from the assigned spotter, except a stop signal, which you follow from anyone.

## What SAFE2GO does

The proximity sensor measures the distance to the nearest object. A WARNING appears when something is closer than 10 m. A CRITICAL alert appears when it is closer than 5 m. The alert clears only when the distance stays clearly above the trigger for a short time.

When a proximity alert appears:

1. Stop movement and hold the controls in neutral.
2. Locate the person or object.
3. Continue only when the area is clear or a spotter confirms it is safe.

## Communicate

Make eye contact with ground workers before moving. Sound the horn before starting and before reversing. If a worker needs to approach the machine, they should signal you first and wait until you lower the attachment and lock out the controls.
