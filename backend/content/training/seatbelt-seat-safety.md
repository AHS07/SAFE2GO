# Seatbelt and seat safety

The seatbelt is the part of the rollover protective structure that you control. The cab frame is built to survive a rollover. It only protects you if you stay inside it. An unbelted operator can be thrown out of the seat, through the door, or into the path of the machine.

## When the belt must be fastened

Fasten the belt before you release the park brake or select a gear. Keep it fastened for as long as the machine can move. SAFE2GO treats the machine as able to move when the park brake is off and a gear is engaged, or when the machine is travelling.

- Fasten the belt before starting work, not after the first cycle.
- Keep it fastened for short moves. Most rollovers happen during short, routine travel.
- Only unfasten after the machine is stopped, the park brake is on, and the controls are locked out.

## Why the system warns you

A short grace period lets you buckle up after you sit down. If the belt stays unfastened while the machine can move, a WARNING appears. If it is still unfastened after a longer period, the alert becomes CRITICAL. Two or more seatbelt incidents in one shift are recorded as a pattern for coaching.

## Stay in the seat

The machine is designed to be controlled from the seat. Leaning out of the door or standing to get a better view puts you outside the protected space and away from the controls.

- If you cannot see the work area, stop and ask for a spotter.
- Never leave the seat while the engine runs and the park brake is off. SAFE2GO raises a CRITICAL alert within seconds if the seat is empty in this state.
- Before you leave the cab, lower attachments to the ground, set the park brake, and engage the control lockout.

## Getting in and out

Many cab injuries happen on the steps, not in the seat.

1. Face the machine when climbing in or out.
2. Keep three points of contact: two hands and one foot, or one hand and two feet.
3. Do not jump from the cab or tracks.
4. Keep steps and handholds clean of mud, ice, and oil.

## If the machine starts to tip

Stay in the seat with the belt fastened. Hold on and brace your feet. Do not try to jump clear. The cab is the safest place during a rollover.
