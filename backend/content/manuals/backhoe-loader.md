# Sample backhoe loader manual

## Daily walk-around inspection

Check tyres, stabiliser pads, and the loader and backhoe linkages. Check the backhoe boom lock and the swing lock. Look for hydraulic leaks at the stabiliser cylinders and the swing cylinders. Check the bucket teeth on both the loader bucket and the backhoe bucket.

## Seat positions

The operator seat turns between the loader position, facing forward, and the backhoe position, facing the rear. Stop the machine and apply the park brake before turning the seat. Make sure the seat locks in position. Fasten the seatbelt again after turning the seat.

## Stabilisers

Lower both stabilisers before digging with the backhoe. The stabilisers take the load off the rear tyres and keep the machine level. Lower the loader bucket to the ground as well, so the machine rests on four points. On soft ground place the stabiliser pads on firm mats. Raise the stabilisers fully before travelling.

## Backhoe boom lock and swing lock

The boom lock holds the backhoe boom in the transport position. The swing lock stops the backhoe swinging sideways. Engage both locks before travelling on roads or across the site. Release both only when the stabilisers are down and you are ready to dig.

## Digging with the backhoe

Start the cut with the bucket teeth at a shallow angle. Curl the bucket as the stick comes in. Take smaller bites in hard or wet ground. Keep the spoil pile back from the edge of the trench, at least 60 cm. Do not dig under the stabilisers.

## Trenching

Dig along a straight line and move the machine back in short steps. Raise the stabilisers before moving and lower them again before digging. Keep people out of the trench unless it is shored or sloped as required by the site plan. Watch for buried services marked on the site plan.

## Loader operation

The front loader is used for loading trucks, backfilling, and moving material. Use the same technique as a wheel loader: approach square, keep the bucket low, and avoid wheel spin. The backhoe must be in the transport position with the locks engaged while using the loader.

## Travelling

Put the backhoe in the transport position and engage the boom and swing locks. Raise the stabilisers. Turn the seat to the loader position. Carry the loader bucket low. Travel at a speed suitable for the ground conditions. The machine can travel up to about 40 km/h on roads in top gear where road use is permitted.

## Shutting down

Park on level ground. Lower the loader bucket and the backhoe bucket to the ground. Apply the park brake. Let the engine idle for a few minutes. Turn the key off and remove it.

## Service intervals

Grease all backhoe and loader pins every 10 hours. Check the stabiliser pads and pins weekly. Change the engine oil every 500 hours. Change the hydraulic oil every 1500 hours.
