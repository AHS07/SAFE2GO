# Sample hydraulic excavator manual

## Daily walk-around inspection

Walk around the machine before the first start of the shift. Check the tracks for damage and correct tension. Look for loose or missing track bolts. Check the boom, stick, and bucket for cracks, especially near welds and pin bosses. Look under the machine for oil, fuel, or coolant leaks. Check the bucket teeth and replace any that are worn past the wear line or missing. Make sure all steps and handholds are clean and secure.

## Fluid level checks

Check fluid levels with the machine on level ground, the engine off, and the attachment lowered. Engine oil must sit between the marks on the dipstick. Check coolant in the expansion tank when the engine is cold. Never open the radiator cap on a hot engine. Check the hydraulic oil sight glass with the stick vertical and the bucket curled in. Top up only with the specified fluid grade.

## Starting the engine

Fasten the seatbelt and make sure the hydraulic lockout lever is in the locked position. Set the throttle dial to low. Turn the key to the on position and wait for the preheat indicator to go out in cold weather. Turn the key to start. Do not crank for more than 15 seconds at a time. Let the engine warm up at low idle for three to five minutes before working. Sound the horn before moving.

## Hydraulic lockout lever

The hydraulic lockout lever disables all joystick and pedal functions. Raise it to the locked position before leaving the seat, before anyone approaches the machine, and before starting the engine. Lower it only when you are seated, belted, and ready to work. If a function moves while the lever is locked, stop the engine and report the fault.

## Joystick control pattern

The left joystick controls swing and the stick. The right joystick controls the boom and the bucket. Check the control pattern label in the cab before working, because some machines are set to a different pattern. Move the joysticks smoothly. Sudden movements cause the machine to rock and reduce stability.

## Work modes and engine speed

The machine has three work modes. Economy mode limits engine speed and is suitable for most digging and loading. Power mode gives maximum hydraulic flow for hard digging and heavy lifting. Lifting mode gives fine control for placing loads. Use economy mode as the default. Auto-idle lowers engine speed when the joysticks are in neutral for a few seconds.

## Automatic engine shutdown

When enabled, automatic engine shutdown stops the engine after a set time with no control input. The display warns before shutdown. Any control input cancels the countdown. The shutdown delay can be set between 5 and 60 minutes by the site supervisor.

## Travelling

Travel with the idlers at the front and the drive sprockets at the rear where possible. Keep the bucket about 30 to 50 cm above the ground. Travel straight up or down slopes. Avoid turning on slopes. Use the low travel speed on rough or soft ground. The maximum travel speed is about 5 km/h in high range.

## Lifting loads

Use only approved lifting points. Check the load chart in the cab for the boom radius and height. Keep the load low and close. Swing slowly. Do not lift over people. Never exceed the rated load shown on the chart.

## Shutting down

Park on firm, level ground. Lower the bucket to the ground. Run the engine at low idle for three to five minutes to cool the turbocharger. Raise the hydraulic lockout lever. Turn the key off and remove it. Unfasten the seatbelt and leave the cab using three points of contact.

## Warning indicators

A red indicator means stop the machine as soon as it is safe and shut down the engine. A yellow indicator means the machine needs attention soon. Common red indicators include low engine oil pressure, high coolant temperature, and high hydraulic oil temperature. A flashing air filter indicator means the filter element is restricted and must be cleaned or replaced.

## High hydraulic oil temperature

If the hydraulic oil temperature indicator comes on, reduce the workload and let the machine run at low idle with no load. Check the oil cooler for dirt or debris. Check the hydraulic oil level. If the temperature does not drop, shut down the machine and report the fault.

## Service intervals

Grease the boom, stick, and bucket pins every 10 operating hours or daily. Change engine oil and filter every 500 hours. Replace the hydraulic return filter every 1000 hours. Replace the hydraulic oil every 2000 hours. The service interval indicator on the display shows hours remaining until the next service.
