# Sample wheel loader manual

## Daily walk-around inspection

Check tyre condition and pressure. Look for cuts, bulges, and embedded rocks. Check the lift arms, bucket, and linkage pins for cracks and wear. Look under the machine for leaks. Check the articulation joint area for damage and make sure the articulation lock bar is stored in its travel position. Clean the windows, mirrors, and camera lenses.

## Articulation lock bar

The articulation lock bar stops the front and rear frames from turning relative to each other. Fit the lock bar before servicing near the articulation joint, before lifting or transporting the machine, and before anyone works between the frames. Remove it and store it before driving, or the machine cannot steer.

## Tyre pressure

Check tyre pressure daily with the tyres cold. Low pressure increases fuel use, causes uneven wear, and reduces stability. High pressure reduces traction and makes the ride harsh. Use the pressure shown on the tyre information plate. Stand to the side of the tyre tread, not in front of it, while inflating.

## Starting the engine

Fasten the seatbelt. Put the transmission direction lever in neutral and apply the park brake. Turn the key to start. The machine will not start unless the direction lever is in neutral. Let the engine warm up at low idle. Sound the horn before moving.

## Transmission and gears

Select the direction with the direction lever or the switch on the joystick. Use first gear for loading and second gear for short carries. Use higher gears only for travel on haul roads. Stop the machine fully before changing direction at high engine speed. The auto shift mode selects gears based on speed and load.

## Loading technique

Approach the pile square with the bucket flat on the ground. Enter the pile in first gear. Raise the lift arms while curling the bucket back as the machine moves forward. Avoid spinning the wheels. Spinning wastes fuel and wears tyres quickly. Back out of the pile with the bucket low.

## Loading trucks

Use a V-shaped or Y-shaped loading pattern with the truck positioned at about 45 degrees to the pile. Keep the travel distance short. Raise the bucket to dump height only as you approach the truck. Dump the load smoothly and spread it evenly across the truck body.

## Economy mode and engine speed

Economy mode limits the maximum engine speed and changes gear earlier. Use it for normal loading. Use power mode only for hard digging or steep ramps. Auto-idle lowers engine speed when the machine is stopped with the controls in neutral. Automatic engine shutdown stops the engine after a set idle time.

## Travelling on roads and ramps

Carry the bucket low, about 40 cm above the ground, with the bucket curled back. Keep speed within site limits. Travel straight up and down ramps. Do not turn on steep ramps. Go down ramps in the same gear you would use to go up.

## Parking and shutdown

Park on level ground. Lower the bucket flat on the ground. Apply the park brake and put the direction lever in neutral. Let the engine idle for three to five minutes. Turn the key off and remove it.

## Warning indicators

A red indicator means stop when it is safe and shut down. Common red indicators include low brake pressure, low engine oil pressure, and high transmission oil temperature. Do not drive the machine with a low brake pressure warning.

## Service intervals

Grease the lift arm and bucket pins every 10 hours. Check the axle oil every 250 hours. Change the engine oil every 500 hours. Change the transmission oil every 1000 hours. Replace the air filter element when the restriction indicator shows red.
