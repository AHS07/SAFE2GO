# Sample site operations guide

## Pre-start briefing

Attend the site briefing before starting work. Confirm your assigned machine, work area, task order, and the location of buried services. Confirm who your spotter is and agree on hand signals. Check the weather forecast for the shift.

## Hand signals

Use the site standard hand signals. Stop is one arm raised with the palm facing forward. Emergency stop is both arms raised. Move toward me is a beckoning motion with both hands. Move away is both palms pushing away. Follow a stop signal from anyone. Follow other signals only from your assigned spotter.

## Exclusion zones

Each working machine has an exclusion zone. For an excavator it is the full swing radius plus one metre. For loaders it is the travel path and the loading area. No one enters an exclusion zone without making eye contact with the operator and receiving a clear signal. The operator lowers the attachment and locks out the controls before anyone approaches.

## Traffic management

Follow the site traffic plan. Keep to marked haul roads. Give way to loaded trucks. Keep a safe following distance behind other machines. Use the horn before starting, before reversing, and at blind corners.

## Refuelling

Refuel only at the designated area with the engine off. Do not smoke or use a phone while refuelling. Clean up spills straight away with the spill kit. Do not overfill the tank.

## Weather and stop-work conditions

Stop work when lightning is within the site limit, when wind makes lifting unsafe, or when visibility is too low to see the work area and your spotter. Resume only when the supervisor confirms conditions are safe. After heavy rain check the ground for soft spots and trench edges for signs of collapse.

## Working near overhead power lines

Keep the machine and any raised attachment outside the minimum approach distance for the line voltage, as marked on the site plan. Use a spotter when working near lines. Assume every line is live until the power company confirms otherwise.

## Reporting incidents and near misses

Report every incident and near miss, even if no one was hurt. Use the manual report button in SAFE2GO or tell your supervisor. Near miss reports help the site fix hazards before someone is injured.

## Blocked tasks and waiting time

If you cannot continue a task for a reason outside your control, such as no truck available or an area not ready, mark the task as blocked in SAFE2GO. Shut the engine down if the wait will be longer than a few minutes. Unblock the task when work can continue.

## End of shift

Park the machine in the designated area. Complete the post-shift check and report any defects. Record engine hours if required. Make sure the machine is locked and the key is returned.
